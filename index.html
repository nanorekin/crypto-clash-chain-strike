How to play

Goal

Win two rounds. A round ends when a fighter's health hits zero or the 60 second timer runs out, in which case the fighter with more health wins.

Controls

One player: A D move, W jump, S block, J punch, K kick, L Chain Strike. Arrow keys with Z X C work too. Walking backwards while an enemy attacks also blocks. Esc pauses.

Two players: Player 1 uses A D W S with F punch, G kick, H Chain Strike. Player 2 uses the arrow keys with , punch, . kick, / Chain Strike.

Touch: on a phone or tablet, on-screen buttons appear under the arena.

Chains

Every hit you land within a short window adds a link to your chain. Each link adds 12% damage, up to 6 links. Getting hit breaks your chain. Punch into kick into punch is the classic route, but long strings of the same move get easier to escape.

Chain Strike

Hits fill your meter. At 100% you can fire a Chain Strike block across the arena. It hits hard and stuns for a long time. Blocking it still costs a little health, and jumping high enough clears it.

Blocking and jumping

Blocking cuts damage by 85% and fills a little meter, but you cannot block in the air. Kicks are low, so a good jump avoids them.

Modes

Arcade ladder sends four opponents at you with rising difficulty, ending with the Genesis boss. Quick fight lets you pick your opponent and difficulty. 2 players is local versus on one keyboard.
